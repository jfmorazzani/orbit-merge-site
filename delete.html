Orbit Merge — Delete your data

The only data Orbit Merge keeps about you outside your phone is what you gave the optional leaderboard: a nickname, a random identifier and your Solo scores (see the privacy policy). Everything else lives on your device and is removed when you uninstall the game.

From the game

Open the game, tap the gear (Settings) at the top of the start screen, then "Leave the leaderboard" and confirm. Your nickname, identifier, scores and any uncollected rewards are deleted from the server immediately and completely. You can join again later with a new nickname.

Without the game

If you have uninstalled the game, send an e-mail to the.captain.corsica@gmail.com with the nickname you used on the leaderboard. We delete the matching entry within 30 days and confirm by e-mail. Since no e-mail address or account is attached to a leaderboard entry, the nickname is the only way to find it; if several players share it, we may ask for the approximate date and score of your last game.

What is deleted

The nickname, the random identifier, every score on the all-time, monthly and daily boards, and any rewards not yet collected. Purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store and are not affected.
