Orbit Merge — Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026. Applies to the Orbit Merge mobile game on Android and iOS.

Summary

Orbit Merge has no account and no sign-in. Your progress (scores, stars, settings, gems and stardust) is saved on your device. The only data the game sends to a server of ours is what the optional leaderboard needs, described below, and only once you have chosen to join it.

Leaderboard (optional)

If you join the leaderboard, the game asks you for a nickname and sends it, together with the scores of your Solo games, to a server we operate on Cloudflare, hosted in Western Europe. The server creates a random identifier for you; it is not linked to your name, e-mail address, Google account or device. The leaderboard shows your nickname and scores to other players. Nothing else is sent: no account, no contacts, no location.

Daily scores are kept for 8 days and monthly scores for 3 months; your best score is kept as long as you stay on the board. Gems earned on the board are recorded on the server until you collect them. You can delete your nickname, identifier and all your scores at any time from Settings → "Leave the leaderboard"; this is immediate and complete. The legal basis for this processing is your consent, given when you join, and withdrawn when you leave.

Third-party services

Two platform services are used, each under its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob shows optional rewarded videos and occasional interstitial ads. To do so the AdMob SDK may process device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location derived from the IP address, and app interaction data for ad delivery and fraud prevention. You can reset or limit the advertising ID in your device settings, and the "Remove ads" purchase turns interstitials off. See Google's advertising policy.
  • Google Play Billing (Android) or App Store In-App Purchase (iOS) process purchases of gems and the "Remove ads" option. Payment details are handled entirely by Google or Apple; the game only receives a confirmation of the purchase.

Data we do not collect

No name, e-mail address, contacts, photos, precise location, or any other personal information. The game does not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs beyond what the platforms provide by default. Outside the leaderboard, nothing leaves your device.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

Uninstalling the game deletes all locally stored progress. Purchases can be restored from the shop on a new device. Ad personalisation is controlled from your device's privacy settings. Leaderboard data is deleted from Settings → "Leave the leaderboard" (uninstalling without doing so leaves the nickname and scores on the board; write to the developer contact to have them removed).
