Orbit Merge — Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026. Applies to the Orbit Merge mobile game on Android and iOS.

Summary

Orbit Merge has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own. The game does not collect, store or transmit personal data. Your progress (scores, stars, settings, gems and stardust) is saved only on your device.

Third-party services

Two platform services are used, each under its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob shows optional rewarded videos and occasional interstitial ads. To do so the AdMob SDK may process device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location derived from the IP address, and app interaction data for ad delivery and fraud prevention. You can reset or limit the advertising ID in your device settings, and the "Remove ads" purchase turns interstitials off. See Google's advertising policy.
  • Google Play Billing (Android) or App Store In-App Purchase (iOS) process purchases of gems and the "Remove ads" option. Payment details are handled entirely by Google or Apple; the game only receives a confirmation of the purchase.

Data we do not collect

No name, e-mail address, contacts, photos, precise location, or any other personal information. The game does not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs beyond what the platforms provide by default.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

Uninstalling the game deletes all locally stored progress. Purchases can be restored from the shop on a new device. Ad personalisation is controlled from your device's privacy settings.
